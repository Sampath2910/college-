# Student User Manual — ProjectShelf

Welcome to **ProjectShelf**, your academic discovery platform for college minor and major technology projects.

---

## 1. Finding Projects

### 1.1 Searching the Catalogue
- Visit `/projects` or use the search bar on the homepage.
- Enter topics, domains, or frameworks (e.g. `machine learning`, `spring boot`, `react native`).
- Instant suggestions will appear as you type.

### 1.2 Multi-Faceted Filtering
- **Categories**: Select from 20 academic categories (e.g. Computer Vision, Full Stack Development).
- **Difficulty**: Filter by `Beginner`, `Intermediate`, or `Advanced`.
- **Technologies**: Filter by specific stacks like Flutter, Python, Java, Docker.
- **Source Platforms**: Choose between GitHub repositories and Kaggle datasets.

---

## 2. Studying Project Blueprints
Click on any project card to open its comprehensive blueprint:
- **System Overview**: Architectural summary explaining what the project does.
- **Problem Statement**: Academic problem description suitable for project synopses.
- **Objectives & Modules**: Structured bullet points highlighting functional deliverables.
- **Original Attribution**: Direct link to the source repository on GitHub or Kaggle, author profile, and licence.

---

## 3. Student Account Features

### 3.1 Bookmarks
- Log in to your student account.
- Click **Save Project** on any project page to add it to your personal bookmarks.
- View all saved projects under `/student/saved`.

### 3.2 Academic Mentorship & Enquiries
- If you need help running the project, fixing database errors, or preparing for your college viva, click **Request Implementation Assistance**.
- Choose your preferred communication channel (**Email** or **WhatsApp**).
- Track quotation status and mentor responses under `/student/enquiries`.

### 3.3 Privacy & Data Rights
- Control your privacy preferences under `/student/settings`.
- Toggle activity tracking, email notifications, or WhatsApp alerts at any time.
- Exercise your right to account erasure with one click.
